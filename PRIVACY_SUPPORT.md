# Lociq Privacy Policy and Support

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Lociq is a minimal iPhone app that shows a city-level demographic snapshot for your current area.

## Summary

- Lociq does not require an account.
- Lociq does not sell personal information.
- Lociq does not use app data for cross-app tracking.
- Location permission is optional and used only to load a local city profile.
- Demographic values and boundary geometry come from public U.S. Census services.
- The latest loaded city profile may be cached locally on your device.

## Location

If location access is granted, Lociq uses your approximate current coordinates to resolve the city, load ACS demographic estimates, and draw the city boundary. Lociq does not request background location access and does not continuously track your location in the background.

If location access is not granted, the app shows a minimal location-disabled state.

## Public Data Services

Lociq uses public government data sources:

- U.S. Census Bureau ACS 5-Year estimates.
- U.S. Census Geocoder.
- TIGERweb / TIGER boundary geometry.

These services may receive lookup coordinates or geographic identifiers so the app can resolve the current city, demographic values, and boundary outline.

## Local Storage

Lociq may store the latest loaded city profile locally on your device so the app can reopen gracefully. Deleting the app removes this local storage according to normal iOS app deletion behavior.

## Support

For app support or privacy questions, use the support contact or support URL provided with the App Store listing or repository page.
