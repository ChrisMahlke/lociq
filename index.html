02 // About LOC IQ

Your city,
on one quiet screen.

LOC IQ shows the U.S. Census profile of the city, town, or census-designated place you're in. No searching, no maps, no clutter: open the app and your city is there.

Built on the Census Bureau's American Community Survey 5-year estimates for 2020–2024, with city outlines from its boundary service.

What it shows:

01The city you're in, with its outline and a dot where you are
02Population, median age, and density
03Median household income, and households
04Homes owned or rented as ten houses, and education as a ring
05Age groups, rent and home values, vacant homes, and commuting, a tap away
06The cities that border yours, a swipe away, each figure beside your own
07Small towns too, labeled when their estimates are less certain
08Dark or light, larger text, VoiceOver, and Increase Contrast, in 12 languages

Details:

San Francisco: ages, rent, home values, vacant homes, and commuting.
